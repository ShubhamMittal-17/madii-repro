One LP solve takes 0.22~s at $n=100$ but 219~s at $n=800$, against 11.0~s for booked-load Dijkstra and 0.62~s for flow augmentation. Booked-load Dijkstra grows fastest among the solver-free routers (up to $n$ Dijkstra runs per round, 18 times the time of flow augmentation at $n=800$). With hour-long rounds a server-class sink can still afford the LP at these sizes, so in the modelled system avoiding the solver buys little. Solver-free coordination matters where the sink is an embedded gateway, the rounds are short, or the network is much larger, and in a distributed implementation, which this study does not evaluate.

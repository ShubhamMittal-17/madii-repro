With the corrected reward, the validation-selected checkpoints of all three seeds reach 8.27 rounds to first death (4.3\% of the fractional bound, legacy accounting). This equals direct transmission to the sink (8.27 rounds): in the first round of every test topology the selected models send every source straight to the sink. This is not what the reward asks for. Scored with the same corrected reward (MADII's Eq.~8), battery-weighted Dijkstra earns 8.1 times more reward than direct transmission in the first round, and 15.3 times more discounted return ($\gamma=0.99$) over an episode. The collapse is therefore a learning failure of the reconstruction (plausibly credit assignment across 100 agents sharing one round return), not a property of the reward.

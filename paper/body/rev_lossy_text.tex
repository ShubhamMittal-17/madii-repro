With lossy links and ARQ, booked-load Dijkstra and flow augmentation live 4.5 and 3.5 points longer than battery Dijkstra, and the LP planner 9.6 points longer, while delivery falls from 99.0\% to 98.3\%, 98.7\% and 94.5\%. With contention added, booked-load Dijkstra and flow augmentation live 4.6 and 3.4 points longer than battery Dijkstra, and the LP planner 11.5 points longer, while delivery falls from 97.7\% to 96.6\%, 97.2\% and 91.6\%. The lifetime ranking of the ideal channel survives losses and contention, but every router that spreads traffic beyond battery Dijkstra's routes pays in delivery, and the more it gains in lifetime, the more it pays: the LP planner most of all. Under lossy links the lifetime gain is therefore a trade-off rather than a free improvement, and the payload delivered before the first death (last column) is the fairer single number.
